IHI Services Overview

Consultancy services provided by IHI

What services does IHI provide?

IHI provides consultancy services for licence application, renewal, and variation for clinics and day procedure centres. We improve operating systems, train staff, design facilities, and prepare documents. We also act as authorised representatives for clients and handle all application procedures and enquiries from the Department of Health. Additionally, we plan and commission new medical centres.

Process & Procedure

Clinic licence requirements under Cap 633

How do I apply for a clinic licence?

A 5-step guide:

  1. Appoint a Chief Medical Executive based on the requirements of the Private Healthcare Facilities Ordinance (Cap. 633).
  2. Set up a system to comply with the Code of Practice for Clinics (2025 edition).
  3. Ensure staff complete the relevant training.
  4. Keep good records of clinic operation.
  5. Submit the clinic licence application documents to the Department of Health.

Initiating IHI's service process

What is the procedure for getting IHI's service?

We visit your medical centre or discuss your needs upon receiving your enquiry. For existing centres, we assess your organisation's size and services before providing a preliminary work schedule.

Project phases for licence application

Will the project be conducted in phases?

The project begins with a briefing and evaluation, followed by analysis and recommendations. Next, we set up policies and procedures, with physical improvements if needed. The final phase is application submission and site inspection preparation.

Licensing Timelines

Timeline for obtaining a licence

How long does it take to obtain a licence?

For new centres, applications must be submitted 2 months before operation. If the required documents are complete, the Department of Health schedules an inspection within 10 days. Follow-up actions may be required after inspection, and results are issued within 15 working days.

Post-licence support and renewal

After obtaining the licence, will IHI continue to provide support?

Yes. IHI supports licence renewal and licence variation applications. A Day Procedure Centre licence is valid for 3 years, while a clinic licence is valid for 5 years. We assist in keeping medical centres up to date with regulations and standards, and prepare them for routine inspections by the Department of Health.

Staff Involvement

Staff members involved in the project

How many staff members from the medical centre need to be involved?

IHI works closely with the Chief Medical Executive (CME) and the operations manager of the medical centre. Other staff need to understand and follow the policies and procedures endorsed by the CME. Some staff will need to complete the relevant training.

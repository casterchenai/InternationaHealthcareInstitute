Insights

Policy updates and news that affect clinic, day procedure centre and hospital projects in Hong Kong and across the Greater Bay Area — summarised from official sources, with links so you can read the originals.

AllHong KongMainland ChinaGreater Bay AreaSubscribe by RSS
  • 15 June 2026
    Hong Kong

    Hong Kong clinic licensing: 118 licences issued, full enforcement targeted for end-2027

    The Health Bureau's June 2026 update to LegCo gives the first hard numbers on the new clinic regime — and a date for the penalties and naming restrictions that will make a licence unavoidable.

  • 14 April 2026
    Hong Kong

    Hong Kong's drug regulator takes shape: primary evaluation begins, CMPR due by end-2026

    Phase 1 of 'primary evaluation' took effect on 31 March 2026, a priority pathway for HA-recommended innovative drugs followed in June, and legislation for a statutory Centre for Medical Products Regulation is before LegCo. What this means for facilities that depend on drug access.

  • 6 April 2026
    Greater Bay Area Mainland China

    Guangdong names 25 international medical service pilot hospitals with direct settlement for Hong Kong insurers

    A two-year provincial pilot pairs leading Guangdong hospitals with Hong Kong insurers so cross-border patients no longer pay first and claim later — and sets a benchmark for what 'international' service means on the mainland.

  • 9 February 2026
    Greater Bay Area

    港澳藥械通 reaches 71 hospitals and a 115-product catalogue: what designation now requires

    The fourth batch of designated hospitals (December 2025) and the 2025 catalogue (February 2026) turn a pilot into a standing programme with a legal footing, faster approvals and a new 'leading product' tier. Here is how a Hong Kong-invested facility positions for designation.

  • 26 January 2026
    Mainland China Greater Bay Area

    Qianhai upgrades its support for Hong Kong and Macao medical institutions — and opens it to foreign investors

    Shenzhen's Qianhai zone has revised the only zone-level scheme dedicated to Hong Kong and Macao medical operators, extended it to foreign and Taiwan investors, and published a 2026 application guide for support of up to RMB 20 million.

  • 22 December 2025
    Mainland China

    Wholly foreign-owned hospitals in China, one year on: Tianjin open, Guangzhou building, Hainan unveiled

    The November 2024 pilot allowing wholly foreign-owned tertiary hospitals in nine regions has produced its first opening, its first approvals in the Greater Bay Area and a set of city-level support measures. A status check for investors weighing the pilot against the CEPA route.

  • 25 August 2025
    Greater Bay Area Hong Kong

    Elderly Health Care Vouchers now accepted across all nine GBA cities: what it does to cross-border demand

    With Jiangmen, Huizhou and Zhaoqing added in August 2025, the Elderly Health Care Voucher pilot covers 21 service points in every mainland GBA city — and the 2026-27 Budget has extended the voucher reward scheme to end-2028. For operators, this is the most direct policy lever on cross-border patient flow.

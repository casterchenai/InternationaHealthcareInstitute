Qianhai upgrades its support for Hong Kong and Macao medical institutions — and opens it to foreign investors

Qianhai's December 2023 measures were the first of their kind. The January 2026 revision (深前海規〔2026〕2號) widens the door and adds a year of results to judge the zone by.

What changed

In January 2026 the Qianhai Authority and Shenzhen Health Commission revised the Measures to Support the Clustered Development of Hong Kong and Macao Medical Institutions in Qianhai. The revision:

  • extends eligibility from Hong Kong and Macao service providers to foreign investors and Taiwan service providers establishing hospitals, outpatient departments or clinics in the zone, including Chinese-medicine and integrated institutions;
  • keeps the headline support of up to RMB 20 million for qualifying institutions, with tiers tied to scale and to the introduction of Hong Kong-standard services;
  • is accompanied by a 2026 application guide for the special fund.

Qianhai also reported its 2025 results: nearly 60,000 cross-border patient visits, 4,045 beds and 14 institutions, the first Hong Kong-owned wholly-invested specialty hospital completed, and the first instant insurance settlement for a Hong Kong patient. Its Plan for Building an International Medical Services Cluster sets 23 tasks across four areas.

Why it matters

Qianhai remains the only mainland zone with a support scheme written specifically for Hong Kong medical operators, and it sits 30 minutes from Hong Kong Island. Opening the scheme to foreign investors is a signal that Shenzhen wants the zone’s medical cluster to compete internationally — consistent with the city’s 26 measures for wholly foreign-owned hospitals, which allow such hospitals outside the municipal facility plan and support their application for 港澳藥械通 designation.

For a Hong Kong investor, the widening is double-edged: more support certainty, more competition for sites and staff.

What to do

  • If you are evaluating Shenzhen, get the current measure text and the 2026 guide, and model the support against your capital plan — the tiers reward Hong Kong-standard service lines, not just beds.
  • Compare Qianhai against Hengqin (Macao-oriented) and Nansha before committing; the mainland investment guide sets out the differences.
  • Design for 港澳藥械通 and insurer direct settlement from the outset; both are now part of what “Qianhai international medical” means.

Hong Kong clinic licensing: 118 licences issued, full enforcement targeted for end-2027

Eight months into the clinic licensing regime under Cap. 633, the Government has set out how far it has got and what comes next. Here is what operators and investors should take from it.

What was announced

At the Legislative Council Panel on Health Services on 12 June 2026, the Health Bureau reported that, as at 1 June, the Department of Health had received 813 clinic licence applications and issued 118 licences, and had issued 2,937 letters of exemption to small practice clinics. Roughly 80% of the clinics the Government estimates to exist had applied for one or the other.

Two forward-looking points matter more than the numbers:

  • The Government intends to bring the penalties for operating an unlicensed clinic into force by the end of 2027, after the transition period for existing clinics.
  • At the same time it intends to commence section 92 and Schedule 8 of the Ordinance, which restrict the use of names such as hospital, clinic and medical centre to licensed or exempted facilities. It has proposed adding terms including 臨床, 醫務, 診症, 診治, 醫治 and specialist-register titles to the schedule.

Why it matters

Until now a clinic that had not applied faced no sanction. From end-2027 it will — and a business that uses “clinic” or “medical centre” in its name or signage without a licence or exemption will be in breach. For investors, the practical consequence is that every new facility should be designed to the Code of Practice from day one, because there is no longer a realistic scenario in which it operates unlicensed.

The throughput figure is also useful. 118 licences from 813 applications in eight months tells you the Department is processing carefully rather than quickly; applications that arrive with complete documents and a compliant premises move fastest.

What to do

  1. Existing clinics that missed the 13 April 2026 provisional-licence deadline should apply for a full licence now. The provisional route was only for clinics in operation on 30 November 2018.
  2. New facilities should apply at least two months before opening, as the Department requests, and should build the premises to the Code before fit-out is signed off.
  3. Check your name. If your trading name or signage uses a term that will be restricted, plan the licence timeline around end-2027.
  4. Small practices run only by the doctor(s) themselves should confirm they qualify for a letter of exemption rather than assuming it.

## The rules, briefly

The *Work Plan for the Pilot Expansion of Opening-up in the Wholly-owned Hospital Sector* (29 November 2024) allows foreign investors to establish **wholly-owned tertiary hospitals** — general, specialty or rehabilitation — in Beijing, Tianjin, Shanghai, Nanjing, Suzhou, Fuzhou, Guangzhou, Shenzhen and Hainan. The conditions that shape a business plan:

- **Staffing:** at least 50% of management and healthcare personnel must be mainland residents; foreign doctors practise under short-term practice permission.
- **Scope:** no traditional Chinese medicine hospitals; no acquisition of public hospitals; no infectious-disease, psychiatric or blood-collection functions.
- **Grade:** tertiary only — which means scale, specialty depth and governance to match.

## Where the projects are

| Site | Investor | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Tianjin | Perennial Holdings (Singapore) | **Opened 26 Feb 2025** — 500 beds, ~RMB 1 bn; first wholly foreign-owned tertiary general hospital in China |
| Guangzhou (Baiyun Station) | Perennial Holdings | Setting approval **2 Jan 2025** in eight working days; 500-bed first phase, RMB 1 bn; operation targeted end-2026 |
| Shenzhen (Qianhai) | Luye Medical | Specialty hospital; business registration completed early 2025; construction progress not yet published |
| Hainan (Boao Lecheng) | Fullerton Group (Singapore) | **Unveiled 17 Dec 2025** — 150-bed tertiary cardiovascular hospital |

Shenzhen has issued **26 supporting measures**, including permitting pilot hospitals outside the municipal medical facility plan, treating them as key industry projects and supporting their application for 港澳藥械通 designation.

## What it means for Hong Kong and Macao investors

The pilot is a route for investors structured as *foreign* entities who want a tertiary hospital. For most Hong Kong and Macao service providers planning clinics, outpatient departments or smaller hospitals in Guangdong, the **CEPA route** remains simpler and broader — and since March 2025 no longer requires three years of substantive business in Hong Kong for most sectors.

The comparison that matters is not "pilot versus CEPA" in the abstract but *which route matches the facility you actually intend to build*. We set the two side by side in the [mainland investment guide](../../greater-bay-area/invest-in-mainland-china/#routes).

Investing in mainland Greater Bay Area healthcare projects

A working guide for Hong Kong and Macao investors: the two market-access routes, where to locate, how approvals run, who can staff the facility, and the cross-border schemes that now shape demand. Last reviewed September 2026. Back to the Greater Bay Area hub.

Why the mainland GBA cities, and why now

Guangdong's nine GBA cities are home to more than 80 million people, and the policy machinery that once treated Hong Kong healthcare as a visitor has spent the last three years building it into the system. By late 2024 there were 43 Hong Kong- and Macao-invested medical institutions in Guangdong. Since then: the wholly foreign-owned hospital pilot opened in Guangzhou, Shenzhen and Hainan; Qianhai's support scheme for Hong Kong and Macao medical institutions was upgraded; 港澳藥械通 reached 71 designated hospitals; the Elderly Health Care Voucher pilot covered all nine cities; and Guangdong named 25 international medical service pilot hospitals with direct settlement for Hong Kong insurers. Demand, supply-side permission and payment rails are aligning at the same time.

Progress under the pilot is real but uneven: Tianjin's Perennial hospital opened in February 2025 as the country's first wholly foreign-owned tertiary general hospital; Guangzhou approved its first (a 500-bed, RMB 1 billion first phase at Baiyun Station) in January 2025 with operation targeted for end-2026; Shenzhen's Luye specialty hospital completed business registration in Qianhai; Hainan unveiled a 150-bed cardiovascular hospital in December 2025. Shenzhen has issued 26 supporting measures, including allowing pilot hospitals outside the municipal facility plan and letting them apply for 港澳藥械通 designation.

Where to locate

  • Qianhai, Shenzhen. The only zone with a dedicated support scheme for Hong Kong and Macao medical institutions, first issued in December 2023 and revised in January 2026 to include foreign and Taiwan investors, with support of up to RMB 20 million for qualifying institutions. Qianhai reported nearly 60,000 cross-border patient visits, 4,045 beds and 14 institutions in 2025, and completed the first Hong Kong-owned specialty hospital and the first instant insurance settlement for a Hong Kong patient.
  • Hengqin, Zhuhai. Special market-access measures directed at Macao providers, and since August 2023 fifteen categories of Macao medical personnel may practise in the zone. The natural base for Macao-led projects and for serving Macao residents.
  • Nansha, Guangzhou. A further special market-access pilot area; the Nansha campus of a leading university hospital was among the first voucher and 港澳藥械通 sites.
  • Other GBA cities. Dongguan, Foshan, Zhongshan, Huizhou, Jiangmen and Zhaoqing all now host voucher service points and 港澳藥械通 hospitals; city-level health commissions have been receptive to Hong Kong-standard operators, particularly in specialties with cross-border demand.

How approvals run

  1. Setting approval (設置審批). Application to the provincial or municipal health commission for approval to set up the institution, with the site, scale, departments and investor credentials. Guangzhou completed this stage for its first pilot hospital in eight working days across three levels of government.
  2. Construction and sign-offs. Design to mainland hospital construction, fire, radiation and environmental standards, which differ from Hong Kong's Code of Practice in several respects (ward and corridor dimensions, fire compartmentation, radiation licensing). This is the long pole in the schedule.
  3. Practice registration (執業登記). Issue of the Medical Institution Practice Licence with the approved clinical departments. Under Guangdong's 2024 rules a licence for an institution with foreign or Hong Kong/Macao capital is valid for five years with annual verification.
  4. Scheme applications. 港澳藥械通 designation, voucher participation, HA pilot collaboration and insurer agreements are applied for after opening, against criteria that should be designed in from the start.

People: doctors, nurses and management

Hong Kong and Macao doctors may register for short-term practice on the mainland under CEPA and may sit for mainland qualification; by late 2024 more than 1,300 Hong Kong and Macao residents held mainland doctor qualifications and 262 were registered for short-term practice in Guangdong. Wholly foreign-owned pilot hospitals must keep at least half of management and healthcare staff as mainland residents. In practice a Hong Kong-led facility runs a bilingual clinical governance model — Hong Kong protocols, mainland registration — and invests early in nursing and administrative training, which is where cross-border quality gaps usually show.

The schemes that now shape demand

  • Elderly Health Care Voucher GBA pilot. Hong Kong residents aged 65 and over can use vouchers at 21 service points across all nine cities (19 institutions, all tertiary grade A or equivalent). The 2026-27 Budget extended the voucher reward pilot to the end of 2028.
  • 港澳藥械通. 71 designated hospitals; 140 Hong Kong drugs and devices approved for use; a 2025 catalogue of 115 products including 16 "leading" items such as surgical robots. Guangdong's regulation of December 2024 cut approval times to 10 working days for catalogue items.
  • Hospital Authority GBA patient pilot. Extended to 31 March 2027, allowing eligible public patients to receive subsidised consultations at a designated collaborating institution.
  • Insurance and data. Guangdong's 25 international medical service pilot hospitals (2026-27) include direct settlement with Hong Kong insurers; a Shenzhen–Hong Kong data channel now feeds authorised records from designated hospitals into Hong Kong's eHealth system.

Where projects go wrong

  • Choosing a zone for the subsidy rather than the patient base and referral network.
  • Designing to Hong Kong standards alone and discovering mainland construction and fire requirements at plan review.
  • Underestimating staffing: the 50% mainland-resident rule for pilot hospitals, nurse ratios and the lead time for doctor registration.
  • Assuming 港澳藥械通 or voucher status comes with the licence — both are separate designations with their own criteria.
  • Weak governance rights in joint ventures, which surface only when quality or pricing decisions are contested.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use the CEPA route or the wholly foreign-owned hospital pilot?

CEPA if you are a Hong Kong or Macao service provider planning an outpatient department, clinic or hospital in Guangdong — it is the established route, and since March 2025 most sectors no longer require three years of substantive business in Hong Kong. The 2024 pilot if you are structured as a foreign investor and want a tertiary general, specialty or rehabilitation hospital in Guangzhou, Shenzhen or Hainan. The scope and staffing conditions differ.

Can Hong Kong doctors practise in my mainland facility?

Yes, via short-term practice registration under CEPA or full mainland qualification. Pilot hospitals must still keep at least half of management and healthcare staff as mainland residents.

What is 港澳藥械通 and can a new facility join it?

It lets designated hospitals in the nine GBA cities use Hong Kong- and Macao-registered drugs and devices not yet approved on the mainland. Designation is granted by Guangdong in batches — 71 institutions by December 2025 — and is applied for after opening.

How long does it take to open?

Setting approval can be fast (Guangzhou: eight working days for its first pilot hospital), but construction, sign-offs, equipment and staffing dominate. A clinic or outpatient department can typically open within a year; a tertiary hospital is a multi-year project.

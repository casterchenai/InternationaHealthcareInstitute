About International Healthcare Institute (IHI)

The International Healthcare Institute (IHI) is a leading consultancy dedicated to supporting healthcare providers in navigating the complexities of licensing, regulatory compliance, and operational excellence. With a deep expertise in healthcare legislation and a thorough understanding of industry standards, IHI empowers clinics, day procedure centres, and healthcare professionals to meet regulatory requirements seamlessly and efficiently.

At IHI, we are committed to delivering high-quality services that prioritise safety, compliance, and patient care. Our team of experienced professionals offers tailored solutions to ensure your healthcare facility operates in full accordance with local regulations, enabling smooth clinic licence application and fostering a culture of continuous improvement in healthcare delivery.

Our Key Services

Clinic Licensing Consultancy / Clinic License Application Support

IHI provides comprehensive clinic licensing consultancy to ensure that your healthcare facility meets all local regulatory standards and is fully equipped to pass government inspections. Our experienced consultants guide you through the entire licensing process, from the initial application to the final approval, ensuring that all documentation is accurate and compliance requirements are met. Whether you are establishing a new clinic or need assistance with renewing your licence, IHI offers expert advice and tailored solutions for your specific needs.

Day Procedure Centre Licensing Support / DPC License Compliance

Our team ensures that your facility is compliant with all necessary safety protocols, operational guidelines, and regulatory standards. From preparing your application to ensuring adherence to updated regulations, we offer step-by-step support to ensure the licensing process is smooth and efficient.

Cap 633 Compliance Audits for Day Procedure Centres

IHI supports our clients with compliance audits under the Private Healthcare Facilities Ordinance (Cap. 633) for Day Procedure Centres.

Healthcare Compliance & Staff Training

At IHI, we understand the critical role of compliance in maintaining a high standard of care. Our healthcare compliance services not only help you understand and implement the latest regulatory requirements but also provide essential staff training. We offer training programmes tailored to meet the unique needs of your healthcare facility, ensuring that your team is well-equipped to uphold safety, quality, and compliance standards.

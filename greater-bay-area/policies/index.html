Greater Bay Area · Policy tracker

Healthcare policy tracker: Hong Kong and the Greater Bay Area

The measures that decide what can be built, who can staff it, which drugs it can use and which patients it can serve — in date order, each linked to the official announcement. Maintained by IHI; last reviewed September 2026. For commentary, see Insights.

What the last two years add up to

Hong Kong has finished building a full licensing code for private facilities and is building its own drug and device regulator. The mainland has opened wholly foreign-owned hospitals in nine regions and widened CEPA for Hong Kong providers. Between the two, Guangdong has wired up the plumbing — drugs, vouchers, insurance settlement and data — that turns cross-border demand into a business case.

Hong Kong

If you are opening in Hong Kong

Clinic licences are being issued now (118 by June 2026). Penalties for unlicensed operation and the naming restrictions are targeted for end-2027, so new facilities should apply from the outset. Drug access is improving through "1+" and primary evaluation.

Mainland China

If you are investing on the mainland

Two routes exist — CEPA for Hong Kong/Macao providers and the 2024 wholly foreign-owned hospital pilot — with Qianhai offering the most specific support. Approval can be quick; construction standards and staffing rules set the real timeline.

Greater Bay Area

If you serve cross-border patients

Voucher acceptance, 港澳藥械通 designation, HA pilot collaboration and insurer direct settlement are separate designations with their own criteria. Design the facility to qualify from day one.

Timeline

Key measures, newest first

Hong Kong Mainland China Greater Bay Area (cross-border)

  1. 12 Jun 2026
    Hong Kong

    Clinic regime: full enforcement targeted for end-2027; naming restrictions to follow

    The Health Bureau told the Legislative Council health panel that 813 clinic licence applications had been received and 118 licences issued, with 2,937 letters of exemption issued to small practice clinics (about 80% of clinics had applied). Penalties for unlicensed clinics are targeted to take effect by the end of 2027, together with section 92 and Schedule 8 restricting terms such as "hospital" and "clinic"; the Government proposes adding 臨床, 醫務, 診症, 診治, 醫治 and specialist-register titles to the schedule.

    Source: Ming Pao report of the LegCo Health Panel, 12 Jun 2026

  2. 8 Jun 2026
    Hong Kong

    Priority review pathway for HA-recommended innovative drugs

    New drugs recommended by the Hospital Authority's Office for Introducing Innovative Drugs and Medical Devices — initially cancer treatments — are reviewed under the "1+" mechanism within 100 working days instead of up to 150.

    Source: HKSAR Government press release on "1+" approvals, 23 Apr 2026; Drug Office "1+" mechanism page

  3. 10 Apr 2026
    Hong Kong

    Legislative proposal for a statutory Centre for Medical Products Regulation; medical device law in preparation

    The Health Bureau briefed the Legislative Council on legislation to establish the Hong Kong Centre for Medical Products Regulation as the statutory regulator of Western and Chinese medicines, and on preparations to place medical devices under statutory registration, with a legislative proposal expected in 2026.

    Source: LegCo Panel on Health Services paper CB(3)261/2026(03)

  4. 2 Apr 2026
    Hong Kong

    Provisional clinic licence window closes 13 April 2026

    The Department of Health reminded operators of clinics in operation on 30 November 2018 that provisional licence applications closed on 13 April 2026. As at 30 March it had received 546 clinic licence applications (193 provisional) and 2,712 exemption requests, and had issued 26 licences, 19 provisional licences and more than 2,600 letters of exemption.

    Source: HKSAR Government press release, 2 Apr 2026

  5. 31 Mar 2026
    Hong Kong

    "Primary evaluation" Phase 1 takes effect

    The Drug Office may now independently assess new drug applications for registered chemical entities seeking extended applications (new indications, strengths or dosage forms) without a prior reference-authority approval — the first step of a roadmap to full primary evaluation by 2030.

    Source: DH timetable for CMPR and phased primary evaluation, 26 Jun 2025; DH presentation to LegCo, Apr 2026

  6. 31 Mar 2026
    Greater Bay Area

    Hospital Authority GBA patient pilot extended to March 2027

    Eligible Hospital Authority patients can continue to choose subsidised consultations at the designated collaborating institution in the Greater Bay Area for a further year.

    Source: news.gov.hk, 31 Mar 2026

  7. 26 Mar 2026
    Greater Bay Area

    Guangdong names 25 international medical service pilot hospitals (2026–2027)

    The provincial health commission, medical insurance bureau and medical products administration published the first batch of pilot hospitals, including Guangdong Provincial People's Hospital and the First Affiliated Hospital of Sun Yat-sen University, with direct-settlement arrangements for Hong Kong insurers to replace "pay first, claim later".

    Source: Guangdong Hong Kong and Macao Affairs Office, 3 Apr 2026

  8. 25 Feb 2026
    Hong Kong

    Budget 2026–27: Elderly Health Care Voucher reward pilot extended to end-2028

    The voucher reward scheme, which adds value when vouchers are used for preventive care, was extended by two years; vouchers remain usable at the GBA pilot institutions.

    Source: Sing Tao report on the 2026–27 Budget

  9. 2 Feb 2026
    Greater Bay Area

    港澳藥械通 catalogue (2025 edition): 115 products

    Guangdong published a new catalogue of clinically urgent Hong Kong and Macao drugs and devices for the nine GBA cities — 45 drugs and 70 devices — introducing for the first time a "leading product" category of 16 items including orphan drugs and surgical robots.

    Source: National Medical Products Administration, 10 Feb 2026

  10. Jan 2026
    Mainland China

    Qianhai upgrades support for Hong Kong and Macao medical institutions

    Shenzhen's Qianhai zone revised its 2023 support measures (深前海規〔2026〕2號), extending eligibility to foreign and Taiwan investors and issuing a 2026 application guide for the special fund, which offers up to RMB 20 million to qualifying institutions. Qianhai reported nearly 60,000 cross-border patient visits, 4,045 beds and 14 institutions in 2025.

    Source: Qianhai Authority; Qianhai 2025 results (Sina Finance, 4 Apr 2026)

  11. Jan 2026
    Greater Bay Area

    Shenzhen–Hong Kong medical records channel goes live

    A secure channel now sends authorised patient data — including images and reports — from two designated Shenzhen hospitals directly into Hong Kong's eHealth system, the first sector to use the cross-border data framework.

    Source: The University of Hong Kong–Shenzhen Hospital

  12. 17 Dec 2025
    Mainland China

    Hainan unveils its first wholly foreign-owned hospital

    Boao Fullerton Cardiovascular Hospital, wholly owned by Singapore's Fullerton Group, was unveiled in the Boao Lecheng medical tourism pilot zone: a 150-bed tertiary specialty hospital.

    Source: Ministry of Commerce (trade in services), Dec 2025

  13. 8 Dec 2025
    Greater Bay Area

    港澳藥械通: fourth batch takes designated hospitals to 71

    Guangdong added 27 institutions (seven in Shenzhen, including Shenzhen People's Hospital and Shenzhen Children's Hospital) and removed one. Hong Kong's Department of Health recorded 63 drugs and 77 devices approved for use under the scheme by 10 December 2025.

    Source: Shenzhen Health Commission; HK Department of Health, Medical Device Division

  14. 18 Nov 2025
    Hong Kong

    Third medical school approved at HKUST

    The Executive Council approved the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology to establish Hong Kong's third medical school, offering a four-year graduate-entry programme with a first intake of 50 students targeted for 2028.

    Source: news.gov.hk, 18 Nov 2025

  15. 13 Oct 2025
    Hong Kong

    Clinic licensing regime commences; Code of Practice for Clinics in force

    The Department of Health began accepting clinic licence applications and requests for letters of exemption for small practice clinics. Clinics in operation on 30 November 2018 could apply for a provisional licence until 13 April 2026; clinics that opened later apply directly for a full licence. The Code requires a Chief Medical Executive registered for at least four years.

    Source: HKSAR Government press release, 13 Oct 2025

  16. 17 Sep 2025
    Hong Kong

    Policy Address 2025: medical products regulator in 2026, next steps for Cap. 633

    The Chief Executive committed to establishing the Hong Kong Centre for Medical Products Regulation in 2026, to consult the Legislative Council in the first half of 2026 on further implementation of the Private Healthcare Facilities Ordinance (premises naming and enforcement against unlicensed persons), and to consult on legislation for private healthcare fee transparency.

    Source: Chief Executive's 2025 Policy Address; Health Bureau press conference, 18 Sep 2025

  17. 18 Aug 2025
    Greater Bay Area

    Elderly Health Care Voucher pilot covers all nine GBA cities

    With the addition of Jiangmen, Huizhou and Zhaoqing, the pilot reached 19 institutions and 21 service points across Guangdong's nine GBA cities, all tertiary grade-A hospitals or equivalent offering general and dental services.

    Source: HKSAR Government press release, 18 Aug 2025

  18. 26 Jun 2025
    Hong Kong

    Timetable for the CMPR and a four-phase roadmap to primary evaluation

    The Department of Health announced that the Centre for Medical Products Regulation would be established by end-2026 and set out phased primary evaluation: Phase 1 in 2026 (extended applications of registered chemical entities), then biologics, then non-first-in-class and advanced therapies, with full implementation by 2030.

    Source: HKSAR Government press release, 26 Jun 2025

  19. 23 May 2025
    Hong Kong

    Commencement notices and Code of Practice for Clinics gazetted

    The Government gazetted the notices bringing the clinic and small practice clinic regime into effect and published the Code of Practice for Clinics, with 13 October 2025 fixed as the start of applications.

    Source: HKSAR Government press release, 21 May 2025

  20. Mar 2025
    Mainland China

    Shenzhen issues 26 measures for wholly foreign-owned hospitals

    Measures include allowing pilot hospitals outside the municipal medical facility plan, treating them as key industry projects, and supporting their application for 港澳藥械通 designation.

    Source: Shenzhen Health Commission

  21. 1 Mar 2025
    Mainland China

    CEPA Amendment Agreement II takes effect

    The second amendment to the CEPA Agreement on Trade in Services removed the requirement for Hong Kong service providers to have three years of substantive business in Hong Kong in most sectors, relaxed equity and scope restrictions, added "Hong Kong law, Hong Kong arbitration" options for Hong Kong investors, and piloted several measures in the nine GBA cities.

    Source: HKSAR Government press release, 28 Feb 2025

  22. 26 Feb 2025
    Mainland China

    First wholly foreign-owned tertiary general hospital opens in Tianjin

    Perennial Holdings' 500-bed Tianjin hospital, with about RMB 1 billion invested, began operating — the first hospital opened under the November 2024 pilot.

    Source: Sina Finance, 31 Mar 2026

  23. 2 Jan 2025
    Mainland China

    Guangzhou approves its first wholly foreign-owned hospital

    Guangdong approved Guangzhou Baiyun Perennial Hospital, a 500-bed tertiary general hospital with a RMB 1 billion first phase at Baiyun Station, completing the setting approval in eight working days; operation is targeted for end-2026.

    Source: Guangzhou Municipal Government

  24. 1 Dec 2024
    Greater Bay Area

    Guangdong regulation on importing Hong Kong and Macao drugs and devices takes effect

    The provincial regulation put 港澳藥械通 on a statutory footing and cut approval times: catalogue items from 25 to 10 working days, non-catalogue urgent items from 35 to 20.

    Source: Greater Bay Area portal policy analysis

  25. 29 Nov 2024
    Mainland China

    Work plan for the wholly foreign-owned hospital pilot published

    Four central departments set out the rules: tertiary general, specialty and rehabilitation hospitals in nine regions (including Guangzhou, Shenzhen and Hainan); at least 50% of management and healthcare personnel to be mainland residents; no traditional Chinese medicine hospitals; no acquisition of public hospitals.

    Source: gov.cn, 30 Nov 2024

  26. 1 Nov 2024
    Hong Kong

    "1+" mechanism extended to all new drugs

    The mechanism introduced in November 2023 for life-threatening conditions was extended to all new chemical and biological entities, new indications, vaccines and advanced therapy products: one reference-authority approval plus local or Asian data.

    Source: Drug Office, Department of Health

  27. 9 Oct 2024
    Mainland China

    CEPA Amendment Agreement II signed

    Hong Kong and the Ministry of Commerce signed the second amendment to the CEPA Agreement on Trade in Services, covering finance, construction, testing, telecommunications, medical and other services, for implementation on 1 March 2025.

    Source: HKSAR Government press release, 9 Oct 2024

Entries summarise official announcements and reputable reports for general information; they are not legal advice and are not exhaustive. Where a measure has been reported but the implementing text is not yet public, we say so. Tell us if you spot an omission: info@interhci.com.
